A page that exists only so the capture smoke has something to shoot.

Every element below is here to be measured: the button gives the smoke an action rect, the headings and list items give it an element map, and the gradient behind them keeps the screenshot from compressing down to nothing.

Steps

  • open — navigate and shoot the result
  • click — press the first match
  • type — fill a field with quoted text
  • check — wait for something to be visible
  • scroll — bring an element into view
  • wait — hold, and shoot nothing

Notes

A failed step keeps its place in the walkthrough, with the reason it failed written next to it, and the run exits non-zero.

Syntax

A step reads <action> <target> — <caption>.

The caption separator is the first em dash in the line, so a target containing one is cut in half — the step still runs, just against the wrong selector.
